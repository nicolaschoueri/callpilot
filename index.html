Questions contractors ask

The setup is meant to be simple.

Do I need to change my business phone number?

No. The intended production setup keeps your existing public number. Eligible calls are forwarded to the CallPilot routing number behind the scenes.

What if I do not use a calendar?

Ava can still collect the customer's preferred day and time and send the owner a complete pending request. She does not pretend the appointment is confirmed.

Can Ava answer only when I miss the call?

Yes. Missed-call, busy-line, overflow and after-hours routing can be configured separately.

What does Ava collect from the caller?

Typical fields include name, callback number, service request, urgency, city or address, and preferred date and time. The exact flow changes by trade and business rules.

Can Ava handle urgent same-day calls?

Yes, when the business enables that rule. Ava can offer configured same-day windows, notify the owner, or transfer the caller. Immediate safety threats are directed to emergency services.

Is the payment on this website real?

No. This is currently a sales demo. The checkout simulates package selection, processing and activation without charging a card or collecting real payment information.
