Phone forwarding setup
Configuration du transfert d'appels

CallPilot is designed so the contractor keeps the existing public number. Exact menus, star codes and forwarding options vary by carrier, mobile plan, PBX or VoIP provider, so the live onboarding instructions must be matched to the actual phone service.

1

Choose when Ava answers

Missed/no-answer, busy, overflow, after-hours, or a supported combination. / Appel manqué, occupé, débordement, hors heures ou une combinaison prise en charge.

2

Assign the business routing profile

Each business receives its own CallPilot routing identity so Ava loads the correct company name, trade, hours, service area, emergency rules and calendar mode.

3

Enable forwarding with the carrier or phone system

The owner follows the instructions for the actual provider. CallPilot should not guess carrier codes because these can differ by plan, device and business phone system.

4

Run a real test call

Call the normal business number, do not answer, and confirm that the call forwards to Ava. Test English/French, caller details, date/time, emergency behavior and owner notification.

5

Go live only after the test passes

The production onboarding checklist should confirm the greeting, routing, hours, transfer destination, calendar mode and notification destination before customer calls are handled.

Demo status / Statut de la démo: the website currently demonstrates this setup. A live CallPilot phone number, carrier forwarding and real telephone test require the production telephony account to be activated.
