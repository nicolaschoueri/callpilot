How usage is counted
AI time is billed by the second — no rounding up to the next minute. 30 seconds = 0.5 minute, 45 seconds = 0.75 minute, and 1 minute 20 seconds = 1.33 minutes. If you exceed your included minutes, Ava keeps answering normally and only the extra usage is billed at your plan’s overage rate.
